# Hausofit - store facts for the DM agent

Edit this file when prices, shipping or policies change. The agent answers ONLY from what is written here.

## Brand
- Hausofit: women's activewear + lifestyle, vintage look. Instagram @hausofit.official.
- Website: https://hausofit.co.il (Hebrew). Orders are placed on the site.
- Lines: FLOW, NOHUT, MIVNI, FORMA, OR SHINI. Also the V70S jumpsuit.
- From the About page: fabrics are chosen carefully after testing comfort, stretch,
  durability and breathability, for intense training and everyday wear; flattering cuts,
  fabrics that feel "like a second skin".
- Business: HAUSOFIT, כביש ראשי 805, סחנין. Phone/WhatsApp 0545020907, email
  info@hausofit.co.il. WhatsApp is answered 24/7 (contact page).

## Prices and stock
- Never from this file. The agent reads them live from the site with its tools,
  so a price change on the site is picked up within 10 minutes.

## Shipping (from the site's FAQ and terms)
- Price: north and center 50 ILS, south 55 ILS, Eilat and the Dead Sea 100 ILS.
- Free shipping on orders over 350 ILS, except Eilat and the Dead Sea.
- Delivery: up to 5 business days, not counting Friday, Saturday, holiday eves and holidays.
- The shipping options for her address are shown on the checkout page; for a special
  destination she can ask before ordering (0545020907).

## Order status and tracking
- She can track her shipment herself: https://hausofit.co.il/order-tracking/ with the
  shipment number or order number (no email needed).
- Otherwise: 0545020907 or info@hausofit.co.il with the order number (the agent hands off).

## Gift for the next order (₪25 welcome credit)
- After a paid order on the site, the thank-you page offers to open an account (just a
  password). She then gets a personal code HAUSOFIT25-<order number> by email:
  ₪25 off her next order, minimum order ₪150, valid 60 days, one use, for her email only,
  one per customer. A reminder email comes after 14 days if unused.
- The agent may mention it once when she is about to order ("وبعد ما تدفعي، بصفحة الشكر
  بتقدري تفتحي حساب وبتاخدي ₪25 هدية لطلبيتك الجاية 🤍"). Never invent other codes.
- If she says she has the code, she enters it at checkout.

## Reviews
- Customers who bought through Instagram or WhatsApp can leave a review here:
  https://hausofit.co.il/review/ (she picks what she bought, stars and an optional comment).
  The agent may send it when she says she loved what she got.

## Payment
- On the site: Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit card, Bit, cash on delivery.

## Returns and exchanges (from the terms page)
- Cancel and return within 14 days of receiving the item, by phone 0545020907, WhatsApp or
  info@hausofit.co.il, with the order number. Returns are coordinated in advance.
- Conditions: not worn (beyond a short try-on), not washed or used, no stains, smells
  (perfume, deodorant, smoke) or hairs, all original tags attached, original packaging if
  possible. Items that don't meet this are sent back to the customer.
- Shipping: the first return or exchange in each order is on us; a further exchange in the
  same order is paid by the customer.
- Refund within 14 days of the cancellation notice, to the original payment method.
- Defective or wrong item: exchange or full refund including shipping, at no cost.
- Customers 65+, people with disabilities and new immigrants (up to 5 years): may cancel
  within 4 months if the sale included a conversation with the business, as the law says.
- Size or colour exchange: subject to stock, coordinated with customer service.
- Cancelling an order: contact customer service as soon as possible (phone or email) with
  the order number (the agent hands off).

## Sizes
- Most items come in XS/S to XL. FLOW and NOHUT go up to XL only (no 2XL/3XL).
- Every product page has a size table and the size finder "רוצה עזרה במידה שלך?". The agent
  uses the same finder through the recommend_size tool (height+weight, bra size or measurements).
